Ring-tailed Lemur

Ring-tailed lemurs live in southwestern Madagascar, in arid, open areas and forests.

Ring-tailed lemurs live in social groups ranging in size from three to 25 individuals. The groups include multiple males and females, and females remain in their birth group for life.

These lemurs travel up to 6 kilometers (3.5 miles) per day in search of food and defend their home range from neighboring groups.

  • Fruits
  • Leaves
  • Flowers
  • Bark
  • Insects

Orangutan

Orangutans are great apes found in the rainforests of Borneo and Sumatra.

Orangutans spend most of their lives in trees, using their long arms and strong hands to swing through the forest canopy. They are highly intelligent and known for their use of tools.

Orangutans live mostly solitary lives, with males maintaining large territories that overlap with several females.

  • Fruits (especially figs and durians)
  • Leaves and bark
  • Insects
  • Flowers
  • Occasionally bird eggs

Green Snake

Green snakes are small, nonvenomous snakes found in North America's grassy areas and forests.

Green snakes are known for their bright green scales that help them blend into vegetation. They are gentle and primarily insectivorous, feeding on small invertebrates.

They are active during the day and are excellent climbers, often seen in shrubs and low trees searching for prey.

  • Crickets
  • Caterpillars
  • Spiders
  • Grasshoppers
  • Moths
